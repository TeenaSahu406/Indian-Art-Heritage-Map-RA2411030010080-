### Description

The Indian Art Heritage Map is an interactive digital map created to explore important locations related to the art history of India.

The map presents different art forms, historical sites, monuments, and regional traditions across India. By selecting a location on the map, users can view its associated art form along with an image, description, and relevant historical or cultural information.

### Objective

The objective of this assignment is to explore the geographic distribution of Indian art and understand how different artistic traditions have developed and influenced various regions of the country.

### Main Features

- Interactive map of India
- Important locations related to Indian art history
- Clickable markers for art locations
- Images related to different art forms and heritage sites
- Historical and cultural information
- Search option for locations and art forms
- Filters based on art period and tradition
- Location cards for easy navigation
- Responsive web design

### Art Traditions Included

The map covers examples from different periods and traditions, such as:

- Prehistoric Art
- Ancient Art
- Medieval Art
- Folk & Tribal Art
- Modern Indian Art

### Technologies Used

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript
- Leaflet.js
- OpenStreetMap

### Project Outcome

This interactive map provides a visual way to learn about India's rich artistic heritage. It connects geographical locations with their respective art traditions and gives users an easy way to explore the historical and cultural diversity of Indian art.

### Deployment

The project can be deployed using GitHub Pages and accessed through a web browser.
